Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 9, 2026

Reading is personal. Here is what Interleaf stores, where it lives and how you can manage it.

  1. Information in your library
  2. Local storage and optional iCloud sync
  3. Export, restore and deletion
  4. Permissions and app lock
  5. Advertising, diagnostics and support email
  6. This website and policy updates

01 · Information in your library

Your library contains the book titles, authors, page counts, spine colors, reading status and completion dates you enter, together with session dates, starting and ending pages, duration, time of day and optional notes. The app also stores preferences such as appearance, reminders and sync settings. No Interleaf account is required.

02 · Local storage and optional iCloud sync

Your library is stored on your device by default. You may enable iCloud sync in the app’s settings to store it in your private Apple CloudKit database and sync between devices using the same Apple account. Apple provides and processes this service. The developer does not receive your library through a developer-operated server. Sync depends on your network, account and system scheduling; it is not a real-time backup.

Turning off in-app sync keeps the local library and does not delete cloud data. While sync is enabled, deletions may propagate to other devices. Before clearing the cloud library, turn off sync on all devices and follow the in-app instructions. System device backups or iCloud backups may also include local app data according to your system settings, separately from the in-app sync switch.

03 · Export, restore and deletion

A complete library backup is a JSON file containing books, sessions, their identifiers, status and related information. Restore merges by identifier: matching items are replaced by the backup, while other existing items remain. Excel import and export exchange reading sessions; they are not a complete library backup.

You choose where to save exported files. If you use a cloud drive or share a file, the service or recipient may process it under their own rules. Deleting in-app data does not delete exported files. Uninstalling the app generally removes local app data but does not automatically clear the iCloud library or external backups. You can view, edit, export and delete entries in the app. The developer cannot recover data without an available backup or synced copy.

04 · Permissions and app lock

Reminders require your permission for local notifications. You can disable them in the app or system settings. The device schedules them without a developer push server. Import and export access files or locations you select through the system file picker. Core reading features do not require contacts, location, camera or photo library access.

The optional app lock uses system device authentication, such as Face ID or your device passcode. Neither the app nor the developer can read biometric data such as your facial scan. With app lock enabled, the app switcher still shows a page preview. The lock authenticates opening or returning to the app; it does not obscure multitasking previews.

05 · Advertising, diagnostics and support email

Interleaf contains no advertising or third-party behavioral tracking SDKs and does not collect your reading habits through a developer-operated server. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, usage or diagnostic information according to your system settings and its policies, and may make corresponding reports available to the developer.

If you voluntarily email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to answer your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending passwords, complete libraries or unnecessary sensitive information. Email service providers, such as Microsoft Outlook, also process messages under their own policies. Contact the address below about accessing, correcting or deleting support correspondence.

06 · This website and policy updates

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies; fonts and images are served with the site. GitHub may process technical information such as visitor IP addresses for hosting, security and logging. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. For Apple services, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

We will update this page and its date when features or data handling change, and request relevant permissions where needed. This policy covers the Interleaf app and this support website. For privacy questions, contact the address below.
