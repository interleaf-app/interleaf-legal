Support

From your first entry to taking your library with you. Find a little help here.

How do I add books and reading sessions?

Tap the plus button in Shelf to add a book. A title is required; author and total pages are optional. Save with the top-right checkmark or cancel with the top-left ×. To log reading, tap the separate record button beside the tabs, or the plus button in a book’s detail page. Choose a book and enter starting and ending pages. Duration, time of day and notes are optional.

How are pages counted? Can I edit past entries?

Pages read are the ending page minus the starting page: page 20 to page 40 counts as 20 pages. Choose a past date in the monthly calendar in Stats to add a session. Open an existing entry to read its full note, then use the entry menu to edit or delete it. Check the date and page numbers before saving.

How do I find a book or mark it finished?

Shelf shows your full library, with reading-status filters, title and author search, and sorting. A book’s detail page shows progress, entry count and total duration. Use the plus button to log reading and the ellipsis menu to edit or delete the book. You can also mark it finished and later resume reading. Separate rereading cycles are not currently tracked.

How do I back up my library? What does restore replace?

Export a complete library backup package (.dujibackup) from More and save it somewhere you choose. It includes books, sessions, styled notes, images and file attachments. Older JSON backups can still be imported. Restore shows a preview, then merges by identifier, updating matching items from the backup and keeping other existing content. Export your current library before restoring.

Is Excel export a complete backup?

No. Excel is for exchanging and organizing reading sessions. It does not fully preserve the library structure, styled notes or attachments. Use a complete library backup package for migration or long-term backup. Review imports and check for duplicates when importing again.

Why isn’t iCloud syncing immediately?

Check that your devices use the same Apple account, that system settings allow Interleaf to use iCloud, and that sync is enabled in Interleaf. Network conditions and system scheduling can cause delays; open the app again later to check. Sync is not a substitute for a separate backup, and deletions can sync too. Avoid uninstalling the app to troubleshoot sync, as you could lose local-only records.

Why didn’t my reminder appear?

Check notification permissions, Focus settings and the reminder time in the app. Reminders are local notifications and skip days with a reading entry. The app schedules a rolling period of up to 14 days and refreshes the schedule when opened again.

Does Face ID hide the app switcher preview?

No. App lock uses system authentication when opening or returning to the app, while the app switcher preview stays visible. Follow the system prompt to use your device passcode when available. The developer cannot bypass device authentication. Check Face ID and passcode settings and contact support if needed; avoid uninstalling the app first.

Does deleting local data also delete cloud data and backups?

Local data, iCloud data and exported files need separate attention. When sync is enabled, in-app deletions may propagate to other devices. Turning off sync does not clear cloud data. Before clearing the cloud library, disable sync on all devices and follow the app’s instructions. Delete exported files separately from Files or your cloud drive.

How do I format notes or add attachments?

Tap Edit Note in a new or existing reading entry to open the full-screen editor. Use the format panel for headings, bold, italics and more. Lists support bullets, dashes and numbers. The attachment menu offers photos, the camera, document scanning, files, web links and dividers. Each attachment can be up to 10 MiB, with a total of 20 MiB and 20 attachments per note.

How is text scanning different from document scanning?

Document scanning keeps a page as an attachment. The new Scan Text flow recognizes text on your device: take or choose a photo, select passages, review the text and insert it. Insert as an excerpt keeps the source; turn it off to insert plain text. Source page numbers are optional and do not change reading progress. Keeping the image is optional too. Screens show the 1.2.2 development version; available features depend on the installed version.

Need a hand? Write to us.

Include your app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots can help; hide anything you don’t want to share. There is no need to send a complete library or any passwords.
