Support

Answers for logging reads, reading spectra, Home Screen widgets, backups, and iCloud sync.

How do I add books and log a read?

Tap "+" in the top-right corner of Library to add a book, enter its title, and pick one of six cover colors (author and total pages are optional). To log a read, tap "+ Log a read" on Today, or tap "Log a read / Scan Page" directly on an active book card or book detail screen.

How are the colors on the Footprints monthly calendar chosen?

Each day on the monthly calendar is colored by your main reading time that day (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Bedtime), while the Year view displays 12 monthly spectrum bands. Tap any past date on the calendar to view or backfill entries.

What is the difference between Excerpt and Echoes?

The Excerpt tab gathers all reading logs that include text, photos, or attachments, with book filters, a Favorites toggle, and search. Echoes on Today randomly surfaces a past note from your history—tap Shuffle to browse another.

Which Home Screen widgets are available, and do they read my notes?

Interleaf includes "Continue Reading" (Small & Medium, filled with your book's color), "Today's Spectrum" (Small, showing today's minutes and spectrum ring), and "Reading Band" (Medium, showing 15-day time-colored bars). Widgets only read book progress and daily page/minute totals from the App Group snapshot—never note text or attachments.

How do full backups work, and what does restoring overwrite?

In More, export a full .dujibackup archive to Files or iCloud Drive. It preserves books, sessions, formatted notes, and image/file attachments (legacy JSON backups are also supported). Restoring shows a preview and merges by ID: matching items are updated from the backup while other existing data is kept.

Can Excel export replace a full backup?

No. Excel is great for reviewing or exchanging reading logs, but it does not preserve rich-text notes or attachments. Use .dujibackup archives for full backups or device transfers.

What if iCloud sync doesn't update immediately?

Make sure all devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is enabled for Interleaf in iOS Settings, and iCloud sync is turned on inside Interleaf. Network and system scheduling can introduce short delays. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting so local-only data isn't lost.

How do I change the palette, app icon, reminders, or App Lock?

In More, choose Light, Dark, or System appearance, pick from four palettes and three Prism app icons, and enable optional bedtime reminders or Face ID / passcode App Lock.

How does Scan Page differ from scanning a document?

Scanning a document saves the page as an attachment. "Scan Page" uses on-device text recognition to extract text from a book page so you can proofread and insert it as a styled excerpt or plain text—without uploading anything to external servers.
