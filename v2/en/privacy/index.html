Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Reading is personal. Here is what Interleaf stores, where your data lives, and how you control it.

  3. Export, Restore & Deletion

  5. Ads, Analytics & Support Emails
  6. Website & Updates

01 · Information Stored

Your library stores the book titles, authors, page counts, cover colors, reading statuses, and completion dates you enter, along with reading session dates, start/end pages, durations, time-of-day slots (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Bedtime), and optional notes. Notes may include formatted text, favorite flags, excerpt sources, links, and photos, scanned documents, or file attachments you choose to add. App preferences such as palette, app icon, appearance, reminders, and sync settings are also saved. No Interleaf account is required.

02 · Local Storage, Widgets & Optional iCloud Sync

Your library is stored locally on your device by default. To power Home Screen widgets ("Continue Reading," "Today's Spectrum," and "Reading Band"), the app writes a lightweight summary snapshot to your local App Group container containing only book titles, authors, progress, and daily page/minute totals—never your note text, excerpts, photos, or attachments.

If you choose to enable iCloud sync in Settings, your library syncs through Apple CloudKit to your own private iCloud database across devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Apple operates this infrastructure; the developer does not receive your library on any developer server. Turning off sync keeps your local library intact and does not delete iCloud data.

03 · Export, Restore & Deletion

Full backups use the .dujibackup package format, preserving books, reading sessions, rich-text notes, and image/file attachments (legacy JSON backups are also supported). Restoring merges by identifier: matching items are updated from the backup while other existing items remain. Excel import/export is intended for tabular reading logs and does not include rich notes or attachments.

Deleting data inside the app does not delete files you previously exported. Uninstalling the app removes local app data but does not automatically erase your iCloud library or external backups.

04 · Permissions & App Lock

Bedtime reminders use local notifications scheduled on-device without a push server. File import and export only access locations you pick through the system file picker. Camera and Photos permissions are requested only when you take a photo, scan a page, or pick images. Page-scan text recognition runs entirely on-device and never uploads pages to third-party OCR services.

Optional App Lock uses system device authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). Neither the app nor the developer can access your biometric data.

05 · Ads, Analytics & Support Emails

Interleaf contains no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs, and does not collect reading analytics on developer servers. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, or diagnostic data according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send solely to investigate and reply. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.

06 · Website & Policy Updates

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages with no analytics scripts, ads, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process visitor technical information such as IP addresses for security and delivery as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. For Apple services, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

We update this page and its effective date when features or data practices change. Contact us below with any privacy questions.
